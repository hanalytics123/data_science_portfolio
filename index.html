Predicting Dengue Cases Using Climate and Environmental Data

This project investigates whether climate and environmental variables can improve one-week-ahead dengue case forecasting for Maynas, Loreto, Peru. Public dengue surveillance data is combined with rainfall, temperature, humidity, wind, pressure and vegetation indicators.
